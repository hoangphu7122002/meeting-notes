# UX research: Wispr Flow, Granola, Gemini "Take notes for me"

Scope: interaction patterns to copy into a local macOS meeting recorder (MeetingNotes). Sources are official docs unless marked. Gaps (not documented) are stated explicitly rather than guessed.

## Wispr Flow (macOS dictation)

- Push-to-talk: hold a key or mouse button, release inserts text. Default hold key is `Fn`; hands-free is `Fn+Space` or double-tap of the hotkey. No-Fn Macs default to `Ctrl+Opt` / `Ctrl+Opt+Space`. https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/6391241694-Use-Flow-hands-free , https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/2612050838-supported-unsupported-keyboard-hotkey-shortcuts
- Shortcuts are user-rebindable (Settings > General > Shortcuts), including Middle Click/Mouse4/Mouse5. https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/2612050838-supported-unsupported-keyboard-hotkey-shortcuts
- Flow Bar: click it to start hands-free; while recording it shows a stop (square) button that ends + pastes, and an X that cancels. Esc also cancels. Cancelling within 0.5s of start is treated as an accidental start. https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/6391241694-Use-Flow-hands-free
- After cancel, a notice offers Undo (re-paste last transcript); a fuller notice with "Open History" is shown at most 3 times total, once per day (anti-nag). https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/6391241694-Use-Flow-hands-free
- Hard limit 20 min: warning at 19 min, auto-stop at 20 and the audio is transcribed, not discarded. https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/6391241694-Use-Flow-hands-free
- Placement (Mac): sits just above the Dock, stays put when Dock auto-hides and on Cmd+Tab, stays above full-screen Spaces (flush to screen edge there), vanishes ~2s while repositioning on full-screen transitions. https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/5002934560-why-is-the-wispr-bar-is-not-appearing-or-disappearing
- Visibility controls: "Show Flow Bar at all times" toggle (Settings > System) and a "Hide for 1 hour" snooze with Undo in the notification. Notch, multi-monitor and hover behavior are NOT documented. https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/5002934560-why-is-the-wispr-bar-is-not-appearing-or-disappearing
- Waveform: row of white bars that rise with input level; same widget in onboarding mic test; start ping sound; bars stop on release, paste is the success signal (unofficial compilation, not pixel-verified). https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@agent-native/core@0.124.6/corpus/templates/clips/desktop/design-refs/wispr-ux.md
- Onboarding (unofficial compilation): browser sign-in, Microphone + Accessibility permission cards with one-line purpose copy, mic test with live meter and device picker, hotkey setup, then a "try it" sandbox practicing the hotkey. https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@agent-native/core@0.124.6/corpus/templates/clips/desktop/design-refs/wispr-ux.md
- Errors: session ends if no audio or mic fails (nothing pasted); input during processing is rejected ("Transcript currently processing"), not queued; failed transcripts get Retry on the error notification and in History (audio kept up to 14 days); Paste Last Text if produced-but-not-pasted. https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/6391241694-Use-Flow-hands-free , https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/2503460374-retry-failed-transcriptions
- Permissions regress after macOS/app updates; dedicated "re-verify permissions" and "Secure Event Input blocks shortcuts" help articles exist. https://docs.wisprflow.ai/collections/6359960513-troubleshooting

## Granola (meeting notes, Mac)

- No bot; captures system audio + microphone locally; nothing records until the user acts (click notification, open meeting after start time, or New Note). https://docs.granola.ai/article/transcription , https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/taking-notes/notifications
- Detection prompts are Granola's own pop-up windows at the top-right, not macOS notifications. Calendar prompt 1 min before start (events with 2+ attendees); clicking opens video link + note and starts transcription. https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/taking-notes/notifications
- Ad-hoc detection: mic-in-use triggers a prompt with a "Take Notes" button; titles vary by app ("Huddle detected", "Call detected", "Meeting detected"); calls starting within 15 min after a calendar event adopt its name. Calendar prompts have a solid left color bar, ad-hoc a dashed one. https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/taking-notes/notifications
- Prompts can be disabled globally or per app in Granola settings; optional "Reposition Granola for meetings" moves the window beside the call. https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/taking-notes/notifications
- Live view: user types a raw notepad; a floating live-meeting indicator appears when you switch to other apps and returns you to the note on click. https://docs.granola.ai/article/transcription
- Transcript panel (waveform icon): grey bubbles left = system audio ("Them"), green right = mic ("Me"); search, copy, delete chunk. https://docs.granola.ai/article/transcription
- Stop vs pause: panel Stop/Resume pauses; bottom stop ends the meeting. Auto-stop when call end detected, after 15 min of silence, or on sleep. A finished note can be resumed and appended. https://docs.granola.ai/article/transcription
- After meeting: enhanced notes auto-generate on end/stop from raw notes + transcript + calendar data. Typed text stays black, AI-added text grey (grey turns black when edited). Magnifier per point shows its source (transcript vs your note). https://docs.granola.ai/article/ai-enhanced-notes , https://www.granola.ai/docs/docs/101/afteryourmeeting/editing-your-enhanced-notes (grey/black per search summary; the first page does not state colors)
- Templates: sparkle button top-right picks a template, regenerate button reruns it; custom templates with own prompt. Re-enhancing regenerates and discards manual edits to enhanced notes. https://docs.granola.ai/article/ai-enhanced-notes
- Errors: if transcription doesn't start, check note open/meeting started, audio devices, restart; audio is not saved, passed to the transcription provider. https://docs.granola.ai/article/transcription

## Google Gemini "Take notes for me" (Meet)

- Start: organizer toggles it in the Calendar event, or in-call "Take notes for me" button at top right, with options (recipients, length, sections, language) then "Start taking notes". https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14754724
- Consent/transparency: Meet announces to all participants and shows a pencil icon; admins can require consent prompts. https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14754724
- Stop: internal participants can stop capture (keeps sensitive talk out); host controls can restrict. https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14754724
- Output: Google Doc in organizer's Drive, attached to the Calendar event, two tabs "Quick notes" (summary + action items) and "Full notes" (summary, decisions, actions, details). Transcript/recording are separate linked files with no jump-to-moment. https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14754724 , https://fellow.ai/blog/google-meet-gemini-ai-note-taker-review (competitor blog, treat as biased)
- Limits: one language per meeting, best for 15 min+; summaries can be incomplete after connectivity issues. Delivery timing is not documented. https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14754724

## Recommendations for MeetingNotes (prioritized)

Effort: S = a few hours in SwiftUI/AppKit, M = a day or two, L = more.

1. **Bottom-center floating pill (NSPanel, non-activating, `.statusBar` level, `canJoinAllSpaces` + `fullScreenAuxiliary`).** Sits just above the Dock like Flow Bar, stays over full-screen Spaces, never steals focus. Because it is not at the top, the notch/hidden menu bar never matters. Also add a menu bar item as secondary entry. [M; the panel config is the only non-trivial part]
2. **Pill states: Idle (tiny dot/capsule, optional) / Armed-prompt / Recording (timer + live level bars + Pause + Stop) / Paused (amber, Resume) / Processing (spinner + "Writing notes...") / Done (check, "Open notes", auto-dismiss ~6s) / Error (red, one action).** Match Wispr's click-to-expand and Granola's stop vs pause distinction. [S per state with a single `enum PillState`]
3. **Hotkey scheme: global toggle (default e.g. `Ctrl+Opt+R`), separate pause toggle, `Esc` = cancel only in an armed/short window.** Meetings are long, so toggle not hold-to-talk; hold-to-talk is wrong here. Make hotkeys rebindable; use `KeyboardShortcuts`-style package or Carbon `RegisterEventHotKey` (no Accessibility needed, unlike Fn detection). [S/M]
4. **Level feedback: 5-9 white capsule bars driven by RMS from the tap, plus a separate small "Me / Them" dual meter** (mic vs system audio, as in Granola's two-sided transcript). Doubles as "is system audio actually captured?" proof. Silence for >10s while recording shows a subtle warning tint. [S with `Canvas`/`TimelineView`]
5. **Hover-to-expand controls**: collapsed pill shows timer + bars only; on hover/click it grows to reveal Pause, Stop, Open notes. Flow Bar requires explicit stop/cancel buttons (click on bar does not stop), which prevents accidental ends. Add confirm-free Stop but a ~0.5s accidental-start cancel and an Undo toast after stop/discard. [S; `.onHover` + `matchedGeometryEffect`]
6. **Meeting auto-detection prompt (Granola model), never auto-record:** custom top-right card, not UNUserNotification. Triggers: calendar event starting in 1 min with 2+ attendees (EventKit), and mic-in-use by a known meeting app (CoreAudio `kAudioDevicePropertyDeviceIsRunningSomewhere` + frontmost/running app bundle ID). Card has "Start notes", "Dismiss", per-app mute; use the event title as note title when a call starts within 15 min of an event. Visually differentiate calendar vs ad-hoc (solid vs dashed bar). [M]
7. **Auto-stop safety rules:** stop on sleep, on detected call end, or after 15 min of silence (Granola); warn 1 min before. Keep audio and process it rather than discard when a limit hits (Wispr 19/20 min rule). [S/M]
8. **Onboarding as a checklist of permission cards with purpose copy and live verification:** Microphone, System Audio Recording (ScreenCaptureKit/audio tap), Calendar (optional), Notifications (optional). Each card shows granted/not state, opens the right System Settings pane, and ends with a "record 5 seconds" test showing the Me/Them meters. Add a persistent "Permissions need attention" pill/menu state because Wispr documents permissions regressing after updates. [M]
9. **During meeting: minimal notepad window (typed raw notes) plus a transcript drawer** with Me/Them bubbles, search, copy. When the app is in the background, the pill is the live indicator (click returns to the note), matching Granola's floating indicator. [M]
10. **After meeting: auto-generate notes on stop, show Processing in the pill and a "Writing notes..." placeholder in the note; user text black, AI text grey (turns black when edited); per-point source link back to transcript; template picker + regenerate button.** Warn before regenerate that edits are lost (or keep a version). [M/L; styling is S, source-linking is L]
11. **Error and recovery states, each with exactly one primary action:** no audio detected (check input / permissions), mic/system-audio permission lost (Open Settings), summarization failed (Retry; transcript is always kept), disk full. Never reject input silently; keep raw audio/transcript until notes succeed (Wispr keeps retry audio 14 days). Show "Processing" rejects as queueing instead of Wispr's reject. [S per error]
12. **Privacy/transparency affordances (cheap trust wins):** red recording dot in pill and menu bar icon at all times while capturing; "Hide for 1 hour" snooze and "Show pill always" toggle for the idle pill (both exist in Wispr); a one-line reminder in the prompt card to tell participants, since local recording has no Meet-style announcement (Gemini shows participants a pencil icon and consent prompts). [S]

Notes on gaps: no source documents Flow Bar hover states, notch handling, or multi-monitor placement; those recommendations are our own design, chosen to sidestep the notch by anchoring at the bottom.
